Policy

Privacy & data

Plain-language summary of what we store, why, and your rights.

What we store

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google user ID, email, display name, and avatar URL — only for letting you sign in and showing your byline on posts.
  • Forum activity: the threads, replies, upvotes, and emoji reactions you create. Public by design.
  • Mod subscriptions: which mods you've followed, so we can notify you about new comments / changelogs.
  • Steam Workshop data we scrape: mod metadata, comments, and changelogs — public on Steam, re-served here for tracking convenience.
  • Cookies: see below.

We do not store passwords (Google handles sign-in), credit-card data (Stripe handles donations), IP addresses (only kept in-memory briefly for rate-limiting), or any tracking pixels / third-party analytics SDKs.

Cookies we set

Cookie Purpose TTL
mb_sessionKeeps you signed in after Google login30 days
mb_adminAdmin sign-in (only on the operator's browser)8 hours
mb_csrfCSRF protection for form submissions30 days
mb_tokenAnonymous identity for upvote-once-per-thread and the post-edit window1 year
mb_oauth_*Short-lived CSRF / PKCE state during Google sign-in10 minutes

All cookies are HttpOnly (not readable by page JavaScript) and Secure (HTTPS only) in production. None of them are used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Your rights

  • Export a JSON dump of everything tied to your account: /auth/me/export
  • Delete your account (anonymizes your posts, removes everything else): /auth/me/delete
  • Question something not covered here: email the operator — see contact below.

Where data lives

The site runs on a self-hosted Postgres database on the operator's hardware in Thailand, fronted by Cloudflare. Google handles OAuth sign-in; Stripe handles donations; UptimeRobot pings /health from its US monitoring servers. No data is sold or shared with advertising networks.

Retention

  • Backup snapshots are kept for 14 days, then deleted.
  • Notifications older than what you read are kept until you clear them.
  • Security audit log (failed admin logins, etc.) is kept for incident response — no PII beyond IP + user-agent.

Steam Workshop content

We re-serve publicly-visible Steam Workshop pages (mod descriptions, comments, changelogs) as a tracking convenience. If you're a Steam user whose comment was scraped and you'd like it removed, contact the operator and we'll add the offending mod to a deny list.

Contact

Open an issue or email the operator at the address listed on the GitHub repository.
